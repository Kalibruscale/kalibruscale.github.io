## 🔍 Features

nghitts comes packed with features to help you create high-quality speech.

- 🌐 **Browser-Based TTS**: Works completely in your browser using advanced Web Workers.
- 🇻🇳 **Vietnamese Language Support**: Understands and converts various forms of Vietnamese text automatically, including:
  - Numbers into words (from 0 to billions)
  - Dates and date ranges
  - Time expressions
  - Currency formats (VND, USD)
  - Percentages and decimals
  - Phone numbers
  - Ordinals
- 🎤 **Multi-Speaker Models**: Choose between different voices for your speech.
- ⚡ **Real-Time Streaming**: Listen as the speech is generated, with no waiting time.
- 🎚️ **Speed Control**: Adjust the speed of speech to fit your needs.
- 📥 **Audio Download**: Save your generated audio as WAV files for easy access.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode**: Toggle between light and dark themes for comfortable reading.
- 📊 **Text Statistics**: Get real-time character and word counts as you type.
- 🔄 **Dynamic Model Loading**: Load speech models on demand for optimized performance.
